# Emes/Truth — Source-to-Argument Matrix

## Root passage

הוּא לְבַדּוֹ הָאֱמֶת וְאֵין לְאַחֵר אֱמֶת כַּאֲמִתָּתוֹ

*He alone is emes, and no other has emes like His emes.*

— Maimonides, Mishneh Torah, Foundations of the Torah 1:4

---

## Three movements

1. **הוּא לְבַדּוֹ הָאֱמֶת** — He alone is emes. Identity, not attribution.
2. **וְאֵין לְאַחֵר אֱמֶת** — No other has emes. Exclusivity.
3. **כַּאֲמִתָּתוֹ** — Like His emes. The asymmetry — same word, different reality.

## The arc

- **Part I (1-3):** The word — what emet means
- **Part II (4-7):** The ceiling — human knowledge is derivative
- **Part III (8-10):** Halakhah — the system below the ceiling
- **Part IV (11-13):** The cost — forgetting the ceiling
- **Part V (14-17):** Living below the ceiling

The root passage returns in the final chapter. Begins and ends with הוּא לְבַדּוֹ הָאֱמֶת.
