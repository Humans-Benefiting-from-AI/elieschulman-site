Emes: Translation Not Truth

About

Elie Schulman

I run Cohort Learning Labs, where I build tools and frameworks for people who teach and lead. I've spent years studying the intersection of Jewish intellectual tradition and modern practice — how ancient texts speak to questions we're still asking, and how translation shapes (and sometimes distorts) the answers.

My books are free because the ideas in them shouldn't have a price gate. If something I wrote changed how you think, that's the return I'm looking for.
