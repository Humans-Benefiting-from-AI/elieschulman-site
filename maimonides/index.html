Maimonides Library

A writing project organized around Rambam's words

The same questions, examined for three audiences. The scholarly track follows Rambam's own writings chronologically. The general and children's tracks are organized by topic — because a reader who is not a scholar does not arrive through the text. They arrive through a question.

Scholarly

Chronological — by Rambam's own writings

Treatise on Logic, Commentary on the Mishnah, Mishneh Torah, Guide for the Perplexed, Letter to Ibn Tibbon. Source texts, translations, and commentary attached to each.
