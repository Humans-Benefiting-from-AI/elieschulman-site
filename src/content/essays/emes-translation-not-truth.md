  <h2>About the excerpt</h2>
  <p>English translations of Maimonides commonly render the Hebrew word אֱמֶת as "truth." The current <em>Emes</em> project treats that as an ontological problem and remains separate from the Ibn Tibbon materials about the process and limits of translation. No unavailable replacement text has been recreated here.</p>
