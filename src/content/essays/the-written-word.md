  <h2>Essays</h2>

  <div class="resource-card">
    <p class="eyebrow">Essay</p>
    <h3>Every Reader Is a Translator</h3>
    <p>Reading is not passive reception. It is an act of translation — the reader's interior work of carrying meaning across. What becomes visible when we notice that every act of reading is an act of carrying meaning across, and that the translator is never a neutral conduit.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-card">
    <p class="eyebrow">Essay</p>
    <h3>Translator's House</h3>
    <p>A companion essay on inwardness and identity through the translator frame. The translator's own house is always at stake in the carrying-across — the person who moves between languages is never unchanged by the work.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-card">
    <p class="eyebrow">Essay Series</p>
    <h3>Dear Fellow Humans: We Have a Large Language Problem</h3>
    <p>A series of essays arguing that humanity's largest problem today is a language problem. The words we share conceal different realities. Before we can solve anything, we need to ask what we mean — and whether those are truly the only meanings available.</p>
