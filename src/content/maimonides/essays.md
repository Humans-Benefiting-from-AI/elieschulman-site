    <h3>Until He Knew — Abraham and the Mishneh Torah</h3>
    <p>Rambam's account of Abraham's discovery: he did not discover God. He discovered that God is not discoverable. The essay follows what that distinction means for knowledge, faith, and the limits of proof.</p>
