The Writing Map

This body of work moves from the inner life of a person, through intimate relationships, into teaching and community. Each section is a relationship that the writing examines — what becomes visible when the structures already organizing how we think and act are brought into view.

The Arc

The writing follows a sequence: from the self, through marriage and family, into teaching and community. This is not a hierarchy. It is a direction of attention — each relationship reveals what the others cannot.
