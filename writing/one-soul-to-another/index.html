Branch 5

One Soul to Another

In the Guide for the Perplexed, Maimonides writes that the word is the only way for one soul to contact another. Every interpersonal relationship is mediated by language — which means every relationship is first a translation problem. This branch examines what happens between souls when the words they share do not guarantee shared meaning.
