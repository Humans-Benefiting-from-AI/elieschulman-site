Reading Paths

These through-lines cut across the relational categories, following a question wherever it leads. Each path is a curated sequence — not a complete survey, but a way into the body of work through a single thread.

Pain & the Cannon

Moving toward the problem. Rabbi Akiva laughed when he heard the cannon, because the sound of suffering was the sound of a door opening. This path follows the through-line of beloved suffering — not as celebration of pain, but as the refusal to turn away from what difficulty reveals.

  1. The Egypt Option — the mind prefers familiar suffering over freedom
  2. עיֵף וְיָגֵעַ — The Weariness That Precedes Every Attack
  3. The Cannon's Call: Rabbi Akiva, Man of Paradox — the anchor essay
  4. The Cannon's Call V2 — suffering as unlikely path to freedom
  5. Beyond Avoidance: Rabbi Akiva and the Wisdom Within Suffering (חביבין יסורין)

Silent Sitting

The practice of pausing before what appears. Not meditation, not mindfulness, not a promise of calm — but a real interval in which there is nothing to accomplish. This path follows the vehicle of silent sitting through its manuscript, essay, and grammar-book forms.

  1. Eyelids Open and Eyelids Closed: The Phenomenology of Seeing and Knowing
  2. שעה אחת קודם — The Quiet Before — the book on the hour before prayer
  3. Combined Grammar Book — verbs, agents, and the illusion of a doer

AI as Catalyst

AI exposes that thought, word, and productivity are not uniquely human. The hardest questions are not technological — they concern what people fear losing, which roles the old system protected, and how a group responds when familiar expertise no longer provides stable ground.

  1. Intelligence Is Artificial — the 15-chapter trade book
  2. Cognitive Labor Consolidated — AI as reorganizer, not replacement
  3. Dear Fellow Humans: We Have a Large Language Problem
  4. Essential Skills for Thriving in the AI Era

Language & Translation

How words carry assumptions and how translation reveals them. This path follows the question of what becomes visible when familiar translations are tested against Hebrew vocabulary, context, and the intellectual worlds in which a text was written.

  1. Emes: Translation Not Truth — Chapter 1 free PDF
  2. Every Reader Is a Translator
  3. Translator's House
  4. Davar: Word and Thing — the third book in the Maimonides series
  5. The Phenomenology of Translation: Ibn Tibon's Philosophical Hebrew
  6. 22 Foundations Ebook Volumes — translation as carrying-across
